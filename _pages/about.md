# 🏖️ About Me
{: #about-me}

I am from Oahu, Hawaii and a current Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Cornell University, where I study Robotics and Controls. Throughout my undergraduate studies and research experiences, I have developed an interest in path planning and control theory, specifically in areas such as optimal and model predictive control, reinforcement learning, and trajectory optimization, particularly for autonomous robots. 

At Cornell, I work with ASML on implementing scalable custom control architecture and simulation for a reticle-handling robotic arm. Additionally, I work in the [GeoDesiC Lab](https://geodesic.mae.cornell.edu/), implementing custom ROS 2 architecture for autonomous target tracking, enabling a small quadrotor to detect and converge onto a moving target. In the lab, I also explore how differential geometry can play a role in control synthesis for robotic systems.

Before Cornell, I graduated **Summa Cum Laude** from the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, where I was fortunate to learn and be supervised by Dr. A. Zachary Trimble in the [RIP Lab](https://rip.eng.hawaii.edu/), working on an autonomous infrastructure inspection robot and an unmanned surface vessel, where I learned about design philosophy, low-level control in ROS 2, and sensor fusion for robust localization in dynamic and GPS-denied environments. I was also active in organizations such as Pi Tau Sigma Honor Society, American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), and Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).
