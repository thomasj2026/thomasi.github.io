# 🏖️ About Me
{: #about-me}

I am a 21 year old from Oahu, Hawaii and a current Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Cornell University, where I study Robotics and Controls. Throughout my undergraduate studies and research experiences I have developed an interest in control theory specifically areas such as optimal and model predictive control, reinforcement learning, and trajectory optimization. 

Before Cornell, I graduated **Summa Cum Laude** (GPA 3.98) from the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering. Where I was fortunate to learn and be superivised by Dr. A Zachary Trimble in the RIP lab working on an autonomous infrastracture inspection robot and unmanned surface vessel.
